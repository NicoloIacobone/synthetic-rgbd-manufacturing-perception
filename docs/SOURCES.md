# Sources

Sources cited by the plan, the assumptions register and the report. IDs `[S-xx]` are stable. Moved out of PLAN.md §9 on 2026-11-03 to keep the per-session context small.

**When to read:** when citing a source, verifying a claim, adding a source, or writing the report.

**Rule:** check every claim against the primary source before it enters the report, and keep the status column up to date.

Status legend:
- ✅ = the lead agent read the primary source and confirmed the specific claim.
- 🔶 = reported by a subagent or a search summary; **verify before citing in the report**.
- ⚠️ = partially verified; the caveat is stated.
