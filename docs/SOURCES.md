# Sources

Sources cited by the plan, the assumptions register and the report. IDs `[S-xx]` are stable. Moved out of PLAN.md §9 on 2026-11-03 to keep the per-session context small.

**When to read:** when citing a source, verifying a claim, adding a source, or writing the report.

**Rule:** check every claim against the primary source before it enters the report, and keep the status column up to date.

Status legend:
- ✅ = the lead agent read the primary source and confirmed the specific claim.
- 🔶 = reported by a subagent or a search summary; **verify before citing in the report**.
- ⚠️ = partially verified; the caveat is stated.

| S-14 | Grunnet-Jepsen, Sweetser, Khuong, Tong, Woodfill, "Subpixel Linearity Improvement for Intel RealSense Depth Camera D400 Series", Intel white paper Rev 1.3 (PDF dated 2019-07-05), https://realsenseai.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/White_Paper_on_Subpixel_Linearity_Improvement-1.pdf (web version https://dev.realsenseai.com/docs/white-paper-subpixel-linearity-improvement-for-intel-realsense-depth-cameras/) | D435 pointed at a textured flat wall and moved over ~3 disparities near 1 m: depth is "NOT linear", with "oscillations of about +/-0.5% at the measured range (~1 m)" for "High Density" and "about twice that" for "High Accuracy"; the "A-factor" reduces the amplitude 3–4×. Names **pixel-locking**: the disparity locks to the nearest discrete value, which lowers the plane-fit RMS ("the RMS error could still … be incorrectly reported as near 0") and gives RMS spikes at half disparities. "The D415 behaves almost identically". Firmware default A = 0 (volatile; reverts to 0 at power loss), while newer JSON presets include the A-factor (FW 5.11.06.250, SDK 2.24.0). D4 ASIC subpixel resolution "may, for example, have 1/32 pixel". Theoretical D435 at A = 0: > ±1.5% at 4 m (High Density), ±3.4% (High Accuracy) | ✅ (PDF text extracted by the lead agent, 2026-11-04). **Caveats:** all numbers are D435; the D415 statement is qualitative; the resolution in Fig. 1 is not stated, so ±0.5% at 1 m is ≈ ±0.11 px (848 px wide) to ≈ ±0.16 px (1280 px wide) for the D435. Our SGBM sawtooth (−0.11 to +0.18 px, A-43) is of **comparable magnitude**; the mechanisms differ, so do not claim "the same behaviour". Whether a real deployment shows locking depends on the preset loaded |
